Monitoring tells teams what is happening within individual technology components, while observability helps organizations gain a broader understanding of system behavior and relationships.

This broader perspective can help IT teams investigate questions such as:

- What is affecting a critical business service?
- Which infrastructure component is contributing to the issue?
- Is the problem limited to one environment?

For large enterprises, this context can make incident investigation more structured and help teams make better operational decisions.
